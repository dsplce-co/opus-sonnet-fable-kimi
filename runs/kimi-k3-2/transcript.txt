## Summary of what I found and did

**Bugs found (by reading, not a provided list):**
- `discontinue()` set state to `"active"` — discontinuation was a no-op (this was the one failing baseline test).
- `reserve()` committed line-by-line → a failing multi-line order stranded partial stock (no atomicity).
- No quantity validation → negative qty *fabricated* stock; NaN/fractional/Infinity corrupted the ledger.
- No `orderId` guard → retrying a reserve double-reserved stock.
- Same-SKU lines within one order were checked independently → oversell possible.
- Discontinued SKUs remained reservable.
- `Catalog.get()`/`add()` and `getMovements()` returned live internal objects → callers could rewrite prices, on-hand quantities, and the ledger itself.
- `Catalog.add()` silently overwrote duplicate SKU ids.
- `lineValue()` did floating-point money math → fractional minor units (33% off 500 = 334.99999999999997).

**Fixes:** two-phase validate-then-commit reserve; strict input validation; per-SKU aggregated demand check; idempotent retry keyed by `orderId` (same lines = no-op, different = throw); defensive copies on all reads; integer money math with documented half-up rounding; input validation on `Catalog.add` and `lineValue`.

**New capabilities:** `cancel(orderId)` — atomic return of all reserved lines, idempotent on repeat, throws on unknown order, works on discontinued SKUs without reviving them. `history(sku, fromSeq, toSeq)` — inclusive seq range, per-SKU, returns copies, validates range and SKU.

**Breaking changes called out in REPORT.md:** reserve now rejects discontinued SKUs, invalid quantities, and conflicting orderId reuse; `Catalog.add` rejects duplicates; read methods return copies; `lineValue` rounds to integers.

**Honestly documented as remaining risks:** no persistence (crash loses all reservations), atomicity rests on single-threaded run-to-completion, unbounded ledger growth with O(n) `available()`, and no cross-instance coordination — all in REPORT.md §2 with what I deliberately left alone and why.
